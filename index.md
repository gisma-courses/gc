gisma-courses provides access to teaching and research content from the gisma Spatial Science Resources working group at the Department of Geography, Philipps-Universität Marburg.

The main goal of this entry page is to make geoinformatics concepts and content available in different formats — from blog posts and individual tutorials to complete courses.

The focus is on reproducible scientific method training and education in the fields of environmental informatics, GIS, remote sensing and modelling.
